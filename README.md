# AmongUs360
Among Us on the Xbox 360 project.

A full port of Among us to the Xbox 360, made 98% by human, very little vibecode used.

HOW TO INSTALL:
1. Extract the AmongUs360 folder.
2. If you are using the XEX version of the game, you can just put the files on a flash drive and plug it into your Xbox, or FTP it over to the Xbox. After this, you can stop.
3. If you are using the Games on Demand version, you can move the game into your Content\0000000000000000 folder.
